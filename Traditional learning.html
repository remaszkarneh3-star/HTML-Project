Why Face to Face Learning is Better


Students in class

In my opinion, F2F learning is much better than online learning because we can see the teacher in person.

1. Better Communication

When we are in class, it is easier to ask questions and talk to our friends. Discussions are more fun and active.

2. Less Distractions

At home, there are many distractions like TV and games. But at school, we can focus more on the lesson.
